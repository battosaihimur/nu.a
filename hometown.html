Sungai Petani Clock Tower

The Sungai Petani clock tower is situated in the city of Sungai Petani, Kuala Muda District, Kedah, Malaysia. It was built to commemorate King George V's Silver Jubilee, marking 25 years on the throne, and was unveiled on 4 June 1936.

Mount Jerai

Natural surroundings perfect for outdoor activities. This is the tallest mountain in the northern region and is surrounded by a vast jungle. 1,217 metres from the sea level, It served as the navigational point for traders from the east and west for ships entering the Straits of Malacca from the north because it is visible from far.
